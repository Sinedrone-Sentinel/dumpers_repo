# Dumper's Repo brand guidelines

**Owner:** Michael Linzenmeyer (RSI: Sinedrone_Sentinel)

## Protected identity

- **Dumper's Repo** (product name)
- **Buy. Craft. Sell.** (slogan)
- Application header branding and logo treatment shipped with this repository (the **Dumper's Repo** product mark)

Registration with a trademark office is not required for these guidelines to
apply. The name is used in commerce by the official site at
[dumpers-repo.com](https://www.dumpers-repo.com).

## Code vs brand

Most of this repository is licensed under **Apache License 2.0** (see [LICENSE](LICENSE)).
That license **does not** grant rights to use the Dumper's Repo name, slogan, or logos.

You may fork and run the software under Apache-2.0, but you must **rebrand**:
do not present a fork as “Dumper's Repo”, “Buy. Craft. Sell.”, or an official
instance of this product without written permission.

The official public site is [dumpers-repo.com](https://www.dumpers-repo.com).

Org logo (PNG) on blueprint detail card flip animations is configured in
**Settings → Site** (super-admin) for the official instance. Do not treat that
upload as permission to use the Dumper's Repo marks on a separate public site.

## Dumper's Fair-Value Price (DFP)

DFP is proprietary to Michael Linzenmeyer and is licensed under [LICENSE.DFP](LICENSE.DFP),
not Apache-2.0. Do not publish, document, or reimplement the formula. Do not
rehost or redistribute the DFP engine outside the official deployment.

Super-admins on the official site may hide DFP prices in the UI; when hidden,
the site footer shows the opt-out notice defined in `src/config/site.ts`.
